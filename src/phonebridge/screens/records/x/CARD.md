# X (com.atebits.Tweetie2)

Use when a task names an X account (x:public). Screen records in this directory are the structured truth; this card is
prose for the task planner (design task-agent §3.5).

## Useful labels
- Home timeline: "For you" / "Following" tabs on top, the blue compose "+" bottom right. The tab bar (Home, Search, Grok,
  notifications, messages) shows only while the timeline is at rest near the top.
- Search tab: a "Search" field on top and topic tabs (For you, Trending, News, Sports); typing lists accounts as the
  name with "@handle" under it.
- Profile: name, @handle, bio, "N Following  N Followers", Follow (or Following), then the Posts / Replies / Media tabs
  and the posts list. Leave a profile with its ← (X ignores the edge swipe there).
- A post: "Post" title with ←, the author, the text, a stats line ("8:00 AM • date • N Views"), the action row (reply,
  repost, like heart, views), "Post your reply" at the bottom.

## Flow notes
- Account by handle: (if the tab bar is hidden, scroll_up first) Search tab → the Search field → type the handle → tap
  the account row whose @handle matches exactly → the profile.
- Latest post: on the profile's Posts list, the first post WITHOUT the "Pinned" label above it → tap its text → the post.
- Like: on the opened post, the heart in its action row (it turns pink once liked).

## Traps
- The tab bar hides as soon as the timeline scrolls; a short scroll_up brings it back. Never tap where it was.
- Pinned posts come first on a profile and carry a "Pinned" label; the newest post is the first one without it.
- Look-alike handles (nasa_fan_page for nasa) are not the account.
- The heart toggles: a pink heart is already liked; tapping it again unlikes.
- Never tap Follow, Message, repost, reply, or the "Post your reply" bar unless the task asks for that.
